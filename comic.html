Section 2 — The Reception Dilemma (Comic Story)

A foreign investor visits AceDev English Corp. Experience the misunderstanding, the rising tension, and the professional bilingual rescue.

Mr. Hans

Silicon Valley Investor
Speaks English natively. Needs to meet the CTO before his 3 PM flight.

Sofia

Front-Desk Receptionist
Friendly and diligent, but freezes when addressed in fast corporate English.

Alex

Full Stack Developer
Bilingual engineer who steps in to communicate fluently and professionally.

Panel 1 — Arrival ⏱ 10:00 AM
👔 Mr. Hans (Investor)
"Good morning! I have a 10:00 AM meeting with your CTO, Mr. Rivera."
"Buenos días. Tengo una reunión a las 10:00 AM con su CTO."
Formula: Present Simple (Have + Scheduled Meeting)
Mr. Hans arrives on time at AceDev reception with a formal business appointment.

Panel 2 — Confusion ⏱ 10:01 AM
💼 Sofia (Receptionist)
"¿Eh? Disculpe... no hablo inglés... Wait, did you say CTO?"
"¿Eh? Disculpe, no hablo inglés... ¿Dijo usted CTO?"
Barrier: Recognition vs. Communication
Sofia recognizes the acronym "CTO", but cannot sustain a basic bilingual conversation.

Panel 3 — Urgency ⏱ 10:04 AM
👔 Mr. Hans (Investor)
"Excuse me, is anyone here fluent in English? My flight leaves at 3:00 PM."
"¿Hay alguien aquí que hable inglés fluido? Mi vuelo sale a las 3:00 PM."
Formula: Modal Polite Question ('Is there anyone who...')
Investor time is scarce. A delay of just five minutes threatens the entire business agenda.

Panel 4 — Turning Point ⏱ 10:06 AM
👔 Investor Reflection
"Clear communication is vital for international software partnerships."
"La comunicación clara es vital para las alianzas de software internacionales."
Concept: Corporate Credibility in Tech
67% of international tech partnerships evaluate communication agility during the first 10 minutes.

Panel 5 — Bilingual Help ⏱ 10:07 AM
💻 Alex (Developer)
"Good morning, sir! Welcome to AceDev. I will assist you right away."
"¡Buenos días, señor! Bienvenido a AceDev. Le asisto de inmediato."
Formula: Future Simple (Will + Immediate Action)
Alex steps in calmly with welcoming English, instantly diffusing the reception friction.

Panel 6 — Resolution ⏱ 10:08 AM
💻 Alex & 👔 Mr. Hans
"Mr. Rivera is in Boardroom B. Please, follow me!" — "Thank you!"
"El Sr. Rivera está en la Sala B. ¡Por favor, acompáñeme!" — "¡Gracias!"
Formula: Formal Direction (Polite Imperative + Follow me)
The meeting begins right on schedule. Sofia is inspired to learn English for receptionists.

Key Reception English Phrases (Guía Práctica)

Essential formulas every software company team member should master when receiving foreign visitors:

"Good morning! Welcome to AceDev."
"¡Buenos días! Bienvenido a AceDev." Ideal warm greeting for any guest or client.
"Who are you meeting with today?"
"¿Con quién tiene su reunión el día de hoy?" Clear polite question to identify the host.
"Please have a seat. I will notify him right away."
"Por favor tome asiento. Le avisaré de inmediato." Essential polite waiting instruction.
"Please follow me to the boardroom."
"Por favor, acompáñeme a la sala de juntas." Professional phrase to guide guests through the office.
